# NBA V13 20:00 CT audit — previous OT × player workload × short rest

**Research only; do not promote.** This is distinct from 19:00's team total/margin experiment. It predicts next-game *appearance-conditional player minutes* after the player logged >=36 minutes in the immediately previous team game, that game went to overtime, and the team had <=1 day off. Earlier Oct 17 workload/rest analysis used a different season and generic 40+ minutes/road back-to-back descriptive groups.

Historical source: original recovered 2022–23, 2023–24, 2024–25 player-stat archives plus V13 game calendar. Quality gate excludes 37/4,947 games (24 play-in/postseason, 3 Cup final, 10 incomplete). All player and team histories strictly precede the target date; same-day batches are not updated until predictions are formed. Baseline minutes = 0.5 × prior-three mean + 0.5 × prior-up-to-ten mean; >=5 previous appearances required. Non-OT heavy-short-rest players supply a control correction. Training-only incremental OT correction is the difference in mean baseline residuals between OT and non-OT heavy-short-rest groups. Training 2022–23 and 2023–24; evaluation 2024–25. Frozen protocol, no parameter search.

Training: 21,433 appearances; 366 exposed, 2,241 matched non-OT control. Common control correction -0.1470 minutes; incremental previous-OT correction -1.8396 minutes.

Holdout: 11,055 eligible appearances, 629 games; only 125 exposed appearances from 42 games.
- Exposed minutes MAE **3.9741 -> 3.7339** (gain +0.2402; 95% game-cluster bootstrap CI **[-0.1075,+0.5927]**).
- All holdout minutes MAE **4.8860 -> 4.8833** (gain +0.0027; CI **[-0.0013,+0.0071]**).
- Control non-OT heavy-short-rest (n=1,057) unchanged by construction.

**No statistically established MAE improvement.** 2024–25 has been examined previously, so this is exploratory. The source is appearance-conditional (no DNP denominator), OT flag is team-level (does not establish the player played OT minutes), and no verified pretip odds, injury or lineup history is available.
